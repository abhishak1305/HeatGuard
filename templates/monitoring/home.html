How It Works: From Telemetry to Public Guidance

1

Weather Telemetry

Current conditions and forecast values come from Open-Meteo. A reading is added to history only when a visitor explicitly saves the provider observation.

2

Heat Index Model

The application uses the preliminary Steadman calculation and, when its screening threshold is met, the Rothfusz regression to estimate apparent temperature.

3

Risk Classification

The Heat Index estimate is mapped to the screening tiers Normal, Caution, Extreme Caution, Danger, and Extreme Danger.

4

Early Preparedness

Users and community operators review actionable precautions, hydration guidelines, and published alerts.

Methodology, Scientific Scope & Limitations

educational

Heat Index vs. Wet Bulb Globe Temperature (WBGT): HeatGuard calculates the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) Heat Index, an apparent temperature metric based on air temperature and relative humidity measured in shaded conditions with light air movement. The Heat Index is not Wet Bulb Globe Temperature (WBGT), which directly integrates solar radiation, angle of the sun, and wind speed.

Applicability Bounds: HeatGuard calculates a preliminary Steadman estimate and averages it with ambient air temperature. When that average reaches about 80 °F (26.7 °C), the application uses the Rothfusz regression and applicable humidity adjustment; below that threshold it uses the preliminary average. This shade-oriented estimate is not WBGT and does not include direct solar exposure. See the National Weather Service method.

Important Legal & Medical Disclaimer: HeatGuard is an educational classroom project. Calculated estimates and visual risk levels do not constitute medical diagnoses, clinical determinations of personal heat vulnerability, or official government disaster declarations. Always heed directions from local emergency services and public health authorities.
